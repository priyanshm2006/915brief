Privacy notice

Version 2026-08-24. In plain language first, details below. Last updated 24 August 2026.

The short version: we collect your email address so we can send you the daily brief, nothing else. We don't sell or share it with advertisers. You can unsubscribe with one click in every email, and you can ask us to delete your data entirely at any time.

What we collect

When you subscribe, we store:

  • Your email address.
  • The date and time you subscribed, and which version of this privacy notice you agreed to at the time — so we can always show you exactly what you consented to.
  • How you subscribed (currently: through the website form).

We do not collect your name, location, device information, or any other personal detail through the subscribe form. We do not use tracking cookies or analytics scripts on this site.

Why we collect it

Solely to send you the Nine Fifteen daily brief by email, and to operate the unsubscribe link. This is the entire purpose — your address is never used for anything else, including no internal marketing lists, no product research, no resale.

Where it's stored

Your email address is stored in a database we control directly (Cloudflare D1), not inside a third-party newsletter platform's account. This is a deliberate choice so that we — not a vendor — hold and control the list at all times.

To actually deliver the email, we use a transactional email sending service (a specialist provider for sending bulk email reliably, in the way most independent newsletters do). That provider processes your email address only to deliver the message and never uses it for any purpose of its own. We'll name the specific provider here once it's live.

How long we keep it

For as long as you're subscribed, plus a short period after you unsubscribe (to honor suppression — so we don't accidentally re-add you). If you ask us to delete your data entirely rather than just unsubscribe, we remove it outright; see "Your rights" below.

Your rights

  • Unsubscribe — one click, in every email, no login or reason required. Takes effect immediately.
  • Access — ask us what data we hold on your address and we'll tell you.
  • Correction — ask us to fix it if anything's wrong (there's very little to get wrong: just your email).
  • Deletion — ask us to remove your data entirely, not just mark you unsubscribed, and we will.

To exercise any of these, email us — see Contact below — from the address in question. We aim to act on requests within a few days.

Security

Your email address is stored in an access-controlled database that only we can reach, over encrypted connections. We don't store passwords, payment details, or any other sensitive data — there's nothing else in the system to protect beyond the address itself.

Consent and this notice

By checking the box on the subscribe form, you agree to this notice as it stands on the date you subscribe (recorded as the "consent version" against your record). If we materially change what we collect or why, we'll update this page and, where required, ask existing subscribers to reconfirm.

Contact

For any question, correction, or deletion request: [your email]. Reply to any Nine Fifteen email works too.

Regulatory note

This notice is written to align with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Nine Fifteen is an independent publication, not a company processing data at scale, and this is a plain-English notice rather than a law-firm-drafted one — but the substance (purpose limitation, no sale of data, working deletion rights, breach transparency if it ever happens) is meant to hold regardless of scale.
