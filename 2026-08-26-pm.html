Post-Close Wrap

Wednesday, 26 August 2026

What happened today, and what it sets up for tomorrow.

Today's Story

What happened. Brent crude fell for a fourth consecutive session, down roughly 2.5–2.9% to around $86 a barrel, after Washington's 24 August sanctions package stopped short of targeting Iran's trading partners and Iran's foreign minister spoke of a possible Strait of Hormuz corridor — and Indian equities gapped up on it, with the Sensex touching 77,987 and the Nifty 24,378. From around midday the gains faded continuously into the close, with both benchmarks finishing at the day's low: the Sensex down 0.24% at 77,472.94 and the Nifty down 0.52% at 24,207.75. The Nifty fell twice as hard as the Sensex because the Sensex carries more bank weight and banks were among the day's gainers, while breadth underneath both indices was actually positive, with advancers outnumbering decliners on both exchanges.

How this reaches India: As oil fell, money rotated out of the defensive, export-facing complex — Nifty IT was the day's worst performer at −1.47%, with Auto, FMCG, realty, consumer durables and oil & gas also lower — and into domestic cyclicals, with banks, metals and cement among the day's gainers; seven of fifteen major NSE sector indices declined overall. Separately, in the derivatives market, September-series rollover data published today by Systematix Institutional Research showed Nifty positions rolling over at 77.4% (above the three-month average for the first time since June) and Bank Nifty at 79.2%, even as market-wide open interest contracted 4.3% — consistent with fewer positions surviving Tuesday's August expiry, but a larger share of those that did being carried forward rather than closed out.

Markets attributed part of today's fade to profit-taking ahead of tonight's US data: the PCE price index, second Q2 GDP estimate, durable goods orders and personal income all land in the same 18:00 IST slot, followed by Nvidia's Q2 results after the US close, around 01:30 IST Thursday — both are likely to set the tone heading into Thursday's session in India.

Macro & Policy

US PCE, Q2 GDP, Durable Goods & Personal Income — 18:00 IST tonight

The Fed's preferred inflation gauge, PCE for July (consensus +0.1% m/m, core ~3.3% y/y), lands in the same slot as the second estimate of Q2 GDP (consensus +1.5%), July durable goods orders (+0.7%) and July personal income (+0.2%) — four prints together typically make the initial market reaction noisier than usual. Indian market commentary cited this release as a reason for today's profit-taking into the close.

Bureau of Economic Analysis schedule

EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report — 20:00 IST tonight

Follows the American Petroleum Institute's reported build of 4.2 million barrels against expectations nearer 0.6 million, which added to today's fall in crude. Given crude's role in today's session and its direct feed into India's import bill, this print is a mechanism to watch rather than a call on price direction.

API; EIA release schedule

RBI Overnight VRRR Auction — Thursday

An overnight variable rate reverse repo auction under the liquidity adjustment facility, announced by the RBI on 25 August, following a two-day VRRR held the same day.

RBI, 25 August notice

Jackson Hole Symposium — 27–29 August

The Kansas City Fed's annual symposium runs Thursday to Saturday; the Fed publishes only dates and a theme, no agenda. Federal Reserve Chairman Kevin Warsh delivers the keynote on Friday at 19:30 IST (10:00 ET), per the Federal Reserve Board's own August calendar — calendar listings still naming Jerome Powell as the speaker are out of date.

Kansas City Fed; Federal Reserve Board calendar

No Domestic Macro Release Today

No release from the RBI, MoSPI, the Press Information Bureau or the commerce ministry was published on 26 August as of 16:30 IST. Today was a settlement holiday for Eid-e-Milad — clearing ran an altered timetable, but NSE, BSE and MCX traded normal hours; it was not a trading holiday.
